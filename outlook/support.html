Online Safety Guard - Outlook add-in

This add-in checks the links in the email you open and shows a safety verdict. It never moves, deletes or labels your mail, and it never sends the email body anywhere - only the links are checked.

Using the add-in

  1. Open a received email and click Analyze with Online Safety Guard.
  2. Paste your add-in key the first time (it is stored in your Outlook roaming settings).
  3. Read the verdict: Safe, Needs review, or Blocked.

Need help?

Contact the person who issued your add-in key. The public site is at safety-guard-web.onrender.com.
